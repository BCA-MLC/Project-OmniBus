# NJ Hackensack School Bus Routing Challenge

High school teams across New Jersey compete to build the best **school bus routing agent** for a real **Hackensack, NJ** road network (OpenStreetMap).

Each morning, buses must pick up students at stops and deliver them to the correct school before start time. Your agent chooses **which stop or school to visit next** while the simulator applies travel times, capacity limits, random disruptions, and fairness penalties.

## Who can join

- NJ high school students (teams of 1–4 recommended)
- No prior RL experience required — start with the simple starter `agent.py`

## What you submit

A **zip file** containing only:

- `agent.py` (required)
- `model.zip` (optional, if you trained a neural network)

See the **participant starter kit** and *How to Submit* guide.
